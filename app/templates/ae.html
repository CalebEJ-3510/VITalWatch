{% endif %} {% call note('About coding and the MedDRA position') %}

Narratives are coded against app/terms.csv, a vocabulary written for this demonstration. MedDRA and WHODrug are the licensed dictionaries a production system would use; we do not have them and do not approximate them, which is why every coded row reads curated as its source.

An event matching nothing confidently stays Uncoded rather than being guessed into the wrong bucket. The suggestion a filer sees while typing is advisory only — it becomes the record's coding solely through a Safety Officer's confirm/correct decision, which is audited.
