    {% call note('What this package is — and is not') %}

    This page is the investigation workflow of this system: the signal or deviation that opened it, the evidence attached at raise, the AI finding awaiting its human disposition, the Company's structured response, the officer's review, Leadership's decision, and the audit rows that prove the sequence. It generalizes the retired single-case investigation board onto the governance state machine that genuinely exists in the schema (§11.4).

    It is not a clinical conclusion. Nothing here infers causation, and no AI finding on this page changed any status by itself — every transition above carries a named human, a timestamp, and a reason.
