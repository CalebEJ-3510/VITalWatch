For a term in one trial, over a 2×2 table — a this term here, b other terms here, c this term elsewhere, d other terms elsewhere: PRR = (a/(a+b)) ÷ (c/(c+d)). Screening threshold PRR ≥ {{ threshold }} with at least {{ case_floor }} cases. The case minimum is not padding — with one case the ratio is arithmetically enormous and epidemiologically meaningless.

Not an incidence rate. The denominator is other reports, not patients exposed. Not causation — a signal is a hypothesis for a human to investigate; causality assessment is a separate clinical judgement on each case. Volumes here are small, so no chi-square or confidence interval is reported: at these counts both would be noise, and the case count is stated plainly instead.

Uncoded events are excluded from both sides of the ratio rather than counted as a category — an absence of information should not quietly depress every ratio in the portfolio. Terms come from app/terms.csv, curated for this demonstration, not MedDRA.
